Case Setup

D — Define the problem

I — Identify facts and evidence

S — Sequence of events

Time What happened Barrier / decision point Evidence

C — Causes and operational reality

Avoid stopping at “human error” or “procedure not followed”. Ask why the system made that behaviour possible or likely.

O — Organisational & Human Factors

Describe how system conditions shaped decisions and behaviour. This is not a judgement of the individual.

V — Verify findings

High confidence should mean the finding is supported by more than one evidence source.

E — Effective Recommendations

Recommendations should be more than actions. They must directly address causes or weak barriers, be strong enough to reduce risk, and define how effectiveness will be proven.

Weak

Brief, remind, email, toolbox talk only.

May not reduce risk

Better

Procedure, checklist, permit prompt, supervisor hold point.

Changes the system

Strong

Engineered control, independent verification, hard barrier, sampled assurance.

Verifies improvement

R — Review effectiveness and reinforce learning
